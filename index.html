Database(s) Status

Enter your Super Admin credentials above, then click Refresh to view database status.

Invoice, Customer and Product numbers are tracked by the app separately from the spreadsheet itself - that's deliberate, it's what lets numbering carry on smoothly across an "Add New Database" switch. If you ever edit a spreadsheet directly (clear rows by hand, test "Make a copy"), those numbers won't automatically notice - click "Refresh Numbering" to re-sync them from what's actually in the sheet(s) without losing any linked archives, or "Reset Numbering & Forget Old Archives" if you no longer need those archives at all. Once a spreadsheet is archived, its invoices become read-only (Find/Edit Bill will show them but won't allow changes) - the same principle as not editing a closed financial year's books.

Change the colors used everywhere in this app - the login screen, sidebar, buttons, page headings, tabs, dashboard/inventory charts and number cards, the Find/Edit highlight box, and the bill header and typography (on screen, print, PDF and email). Every option shows a live preview next to it. Changes apply for every biller and device the moment you click Save. This only ever changes colors, fonts and layout - nothing here can affect how the app works.

As Super Admin, you can fix this immediately - this creates a new database, carries your Billers, Products and Customers over automatically, and archives the current (full) database as read-only. Enter your Super Admin username/password in Admin Settings first if you haven't already this session.
